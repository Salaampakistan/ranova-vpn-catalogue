RANOVA Standalone VPN Engine Test

This engineering test embeds the io.github.tim06 OpenVPN Android library and
does not require a separately installed VPN application.

The library artifact declares Apache-2.0 in Maven Central. Its OpenVPN lineage
also includes open-source components with their own license obligations.
RANOVA source for this test is kept in this public repository.

This test is not the final production RANOVA VPN PRO release.
